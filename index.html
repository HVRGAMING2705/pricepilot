PricePilot

Used Car Price Predictor — ridge regression (alpha = 1.0), trained on 600 synthetic car listings. All prediction happens in your browser; no data leaves this page.

Enter the car details and press Predict price.

Model: 5-fold cross-validated R² = 0.745, mean absolute error = ₹1.79 lakh (baseline guessing the average price: ₹3.62 lakh — about 2× better). Predictions are capped between ₹0.50 lakh and ₹80 lakh. Trained on synthetic data for learning purposes; real prices also depend on condition, service history and local demand.
